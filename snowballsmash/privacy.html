This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use Snowball Smash (the “App”, the “Game”), developed and published by Karen Gyurjyan (“we”, “us”, the “Developer”), how that information is used, and what choices you have.

By downloading or using the App, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

The current version of Snowball Smash contains no advertisements and no in-app purchases. In-game currency is earned only by playing and has no real-world value. Future versions may introduce advertising and related measurement; the sections below describe how such data would be handled, and the data safety / privacy label on the App's store listing always reflects the specific version you install.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide directly

The App does not ask for your real name, email address, phone number, postal address, or payment details. You may optionally choose a display name to show on the leaderboards; if you do, we store that name. If you contact us by email for support or a privacy request, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message, and use it only to answer you.

Information collected automatically

To provide online features (saving your progress, syncing across sessions, and leaderboards), the App uses Google Firebase and automatically collects:

  • An anonymous account identifier — created automatically by Firebase Authentication so your progress can be saved. It is a random identifier and is not linked to your real-world identity.
  • Game data — your level progress, stars earned, in-game currency, owned cosmetic items (skins), and settings, stored against your anonymous identifier.
  • Approximate region — your country, derived from your IP address, used for leaderboard context. The App does not collect precise GPS location.
  • Usage and event data — via Firebase Analytics: session activity, levels started and completed, scores, and features used.
  • Device and technical information — device model, operating system and version, language, and country or region.
  • Crash and diagnostic data — via Firebase Crashlytics: a technical report if the App crashes or misbehaves, which may include device information, the App's state, and the date and time.

Information collected in versions that include advertising

The current version does not do this, but a future version of the App may be supported by advertising and may measure the performance of our own marketing. In that case, and subject to the consent choices you make, the App may also collect:

  • Device advertising identifiers — the Google Advertising ID (GAID) on Android or the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) on iOS. These are resettable identifiers you control in your device settings.
  • Advertising and attribution data — which ads were shown, whether an ad was viewed, clicked, or closed, and whether an app install or event followed an ad.

2. How we use information

  • To operate the App and save and sync your progress.
  • To provide leaderboards.
  • To diagnose crashes and technical faults, and to improve stability and performance.
  • To understand how players use the App so we can improve level design, difficulty, and overall gameplay.
  • To detect and prevent cheating, fraud, and abuse.
  • In versions that include advertising: to display ads (including personalised ads where you have consented), to measure how our advertising performs, and to prevent invalid or fraudulent ad activity.
  • To meet our legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we process your personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Legitimate interests — for operating the game's online features, saving progress, leaderboards, security, crash reporting, and analytics needed to keep the App working and to improve it.
  • Consent — for personalised advertising and any non-essential analytics in versions that use them. Where consent is required, you will be shown a consent request, and you can change your choice at any time in your device settings or the App's settings.
  • Legal obligation — where we are required by law to retain or disclose information.

The App uses the following services provided by Google, which process data as our service provider under Google's own privacy terms. We encourage you to read them.

A future, ad-supported version of the App may also use advertising, mediation, and attribution partners, which act as independent data controllers for the data they collect. These may include:

  • Meta Platforms (Facebook SDK / Audience Network) — analytics, attribution, and advertising

Not every service listed above is present in every version of the App. Firebase is used in all versions; advertising and attribution partners are only present in versions that include advertising. The data safety section (Google Play) or privacy label (App Store) on the store listing always describes what the version you install actually collects.

5. Advertising and your choices

The current version of the App shows no ads. If a future version is ad-supported, advertising may be personalised (selected using your advertising identifier and inferred interests) or non-personalised (based only on general context such as your approximate country). You can control this:

  • Android — open Settings → Google → Ads to delete your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation.
  • iOS — open Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and turn off “Allow Apps to Request to Track”, or decline the App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • In the App — where a consent screen is shown, you can reopen it from the App's settings and change your choice.

Turning off personalised advertising does not remove ads; it means the ads you see will be less relevant to you.

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable minimum age in your country, which is 16 in some parts of the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below that age. All accounts are anonymous.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the email address at the top of this page and we will delete it promptly.

Our service providers apply their own retention periods, described in their privacy policies.
