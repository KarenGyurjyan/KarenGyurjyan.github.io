1. Information we collect

Information you provide directly

Our Apps do not require you to create an account, and we do not ask you to submit your name, postal address, phone number, or payment card details. Any settings, progress, or preferences you set inside the App are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to us.

If you contact us by email for support or to make a privacy request, we will receive your email address and whatever information you choose to include in your message. We use that information only to answer you.

Information collected automatically

The App uses third-party services that collect certain information automatically. Depending on the App and on the consent choices you make, this may include:

  • Device advertising identifiers — the Google Advertising ID (GAID) on Android, or the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) on iOS. These are resettable identifiers that you control in your device settings.
  • Device and technical information — device model, manufacturer, operating system and version, screen size, language, country or region, mobile network, and time zone.
  • Usage and event data — session start and end times, levels started and completed, in-app screens viewed, features used, purchases made, and similar gameplay events.
  • Advertising data — which ads were shown to you, whether an ad was viewed, clicked, or closed, and whether an app install followed an ad.
  • Approximate location — derived from your IP address at country or city level. Our Apps do not collect precise GPS location.
  • Crash and diagnostic data — if the App crashes or behaves unexpectedly, we collect a technical report describing the state of the App at that moment, including IP address, device name, operating system version, the App's configuration, and the date and time.

  • To operate the App and deliver its features.

  • To detect and prevent fraud, invalid ad traffic, cheating, and abuse.
  • To meet our legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

5. Advertising and your choices

Our Apps are free and are supported by advertising. Advertising may be personalised, meaning it is selected based on your advertising identifier and inferred interests, or non-personalised, meaning it is based only on general context such as the App you are using and your approximate country.

Controlling personalised advertising

  • Android — open Settings → Google → Ads, where you can delete your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation.
  • iOS — open Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and turn off “Allow Apps to Request to Track”, or decline the App Tracking Transparency prompt shown when you first open the App.
  • In the App — where a consent screen was shown to you, you can reopen it from the App's settings or privacy menu and change your choice.

Turning off personalised advertising does not remove ads from the App; it means the ads you see will be less relevant to you.
